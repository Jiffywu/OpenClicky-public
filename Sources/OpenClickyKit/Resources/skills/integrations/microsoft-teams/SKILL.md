# Microsoft Teams

## Where the tools are

Use the Teams server named in the job's "Connected apps" section, or search the `composio` server's tools for
"teams" (names start with `MICROSOFT_TEAMS_`). If neither is listed, Teams isn't connected. Only work or school
accounts work; personal Teams accounts can't be connected.

## Finding conversations

Teams and channels, and one-to-one or group chats, are addressed by id. List the user's teams, then the team's
channels, or list their chats, and pick by name. Read recent messages in pages; stop once you have what the
question needs.

## Meetings

Meeting transcripts and recordings are available only when the meeting was transcribed and the user's account may
see it. If there's none, say so and summarize the meeting chat instead.

## Posting

Write the exact message, show it to the user in your report with the channel or chat it would go to, and post it
only after they approve that text. Mentions notify people; include them only when asked.

## Before you report

Read the channel or chat again and confirm your message is there as approved.
