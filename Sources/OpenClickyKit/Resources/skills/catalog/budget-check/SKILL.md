# Budget check

## Working with the data

- Read the export as data (CSV, spreadsheet or PDF text) and keep the original file unchanged.
- Put each transaction in one category: housing, groceries, eating out, transport, subscriptions, shopping, bills,
  travel, health, other. Keep a "check" category for anything unclear rather than guessing.
- Total by category and by month with a script, not by eye, and say how many transactions the totals cover.

## What to point out

- Recurring charges (same merchant, similar amount, regular interval), with the yearly cost.
- Amounts well above the usual for that category or merchant.
- Possible duplicates.

## Advice

Two or three specific, optional suggestions tied to the numbers ("three streaming services cost £41 a month").
No lectures, and never move money or cancel anything yourself.
