# Trip planner

## Before planning

Find out (or infer) dates, who is travelling, pace (relaxed or packed), budget and must-sees. Ask only for what
truly changes the plan.

## The itinerary

- Group sights by area so each day has little back-and-forth.
- Check opening days and hours, and how long it takes to get between stops. Note when a place needs a timed ticket.
- Leave about a fifth of each day free, and put the most important thing early.
- Give each day a short title, the stops in order with times, and where to eat nearby.

## The checklist

End with what to book and by when (flights, trains, timed entries, restaurants), documents needed, and anything
seasonal (weather, holidays, strikes) worth checking closer to the date. Say which details you verified and when.
