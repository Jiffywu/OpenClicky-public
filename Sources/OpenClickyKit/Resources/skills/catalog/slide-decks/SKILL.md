# Slide decks

## Story first

Before any slide, write the deck's single takeaway and its ask in one sentence each. Then outline: context, the
problem, what we found or propose, evidence, the ask. Every slide must earn its place in that line.

## Each slide

- The title is a full-sentence headline that states the point ("Churn halved after the onboarding change"), not
  a label ("Churn").
- One idea per slide. If it needs two, it's two slides.
- At most about 30 words of body text; put the detail in presenter notes.
- Charts: one message each, label the line that matters, drop gridlines and legends you don't need.

## In Keynote

Use the theme's master slides and styles rather than hand-formatting, keep fonts to the theme's two, and align to
the guides. Put sources in small text at the bottom of the slide they support.

## Finish

End with the ask and the next step, not "Questions?".
