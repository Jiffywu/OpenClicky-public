# Research brief

## Method

1. Restate the question in one line and decide what would answer it.
2. Find at least three independent sources; prefer primary ones (official docs, filings, papers, the company's own
   pages) over summaries of summaries.
3. Note each source's date. Prefer recent ones for anything that changes (prices, versions, laws).
4. Where sources disagree, say so and say which you trust more and why.

## The brief

- **Answer**: two or three sentences that answer the question directly.
- **What we know**: short bullets, each with its source linked.
- **What's uncertain**: gaps, conflicting claims, things that couldn't be checked.
- **Sources**: the list, with dates.

## Never

Invent a source, a quote or a number. If you couldn't find something, say so.
