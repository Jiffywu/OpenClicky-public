# Meeting notes

## Shape of the result

1. **Summary**: two or three sentences on what the meeting was for and where it landed.
2. **Decisions**: one line each, in the words people agreed on.
3. **Action items**: `- [ ] Owner: task (due date)`. Use the names as written. Leave "owner?" or "date?" when
   nobody said, rather than guessing.
4. **Open questions**: things raised but not settled.

## Rules

- Only what was said. If the notes are ambiguous, flag it ("unclear whether Sam or Priya owns this").
- Keep numbers, dates and names exactly as given.
- Short beats complete: drop small talk and repeated points.
- When the user wants to send the notes on, offer a version written for the attendees and leave sending to them.
